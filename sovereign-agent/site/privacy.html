Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026
Contact: admin@ariasolutions.org

This policy explains what data BigKev's Bot Shop (our Discord server and bots, including "Aria") handles, and how. We built the Service local-first and privacy-lean on purpose — the honest summary is: we keep very little, and we sell nothing.

What we collect

What we do NOT collect

  • No payment card data. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe; we see subscription status and the email you give Stripe, never card numbers.
  • No message content from ordinary channels. The bots only process messages sent to them (slash commands, the ask channel, DMs).
  • No tracking pixels, no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers.

How long we keep it

Audit logs (who asked what, when, and moderation records) are kept only as long as needed for operating and protecting the Service, then compacted or deleted. Cancelling your subscription removes your subscriber role; you may additionally request deletion of your stored interaction data (see below).

Who we share it with

Nobody, except: Discord (the platform the Service runs on), Stripe (payments), and where required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data — ever.

AI processing

Some replies are generated by a locally hosted language model on the operator's own hardware. Your questions to the bots are processed on that machine; they are not sent to third-party AI providers.

Your choices

  • Access / deletion: email admin@ariasolutions.org (or ask in #order-here) to request a copy or deletion of interaction data tied to your Discord ID.
  • DMs: don't want the welcome DM? Disable DMs from server members; everything works without it.
  • Leaving the server ends all live data collection about you.

Children

The Service follows Discord's minimum age requirements and is not directed at children below them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in #announcements with a new effective date.

Written in good faith, in plain language, and not yet reviewed by an attorney.
