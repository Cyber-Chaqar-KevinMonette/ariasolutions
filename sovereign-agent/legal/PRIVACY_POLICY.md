# BigKev's Bot Shop — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 14, 2026
**Contact:** admin@ariasolutions.org

This policy explains what data **BigKev's Bot Shop** (our Discord server
and bots, including "Aria") handles, and how. We built the Service
local-first and privacy-lean on purpose — the honest summary is: **we keep
very little, and we sell nothing.**

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Your Discord user ID and username | to answer you, run subscriptions/roles, prevent abuse (rate-limits), and greet new members exactly once | operator's private machine (audit logs) |
| Messages you send TO our bots (`/ask`, #ask-aria, DMs to the bot) | to generate the reply; a short excerpt is kept in an audit log for abuse prevention and quality | operator's private machine |
| Order info you share in `#order-here` (e.g., which bot you want) | to set up your service | Discord + operator's private machine |
| Subscription status (active/cancelled — via Stripe) | to grant/revoke subscriber roles | Stripe; role state in Discord |

## What we do NOT collect

- **No payment card data.** Payments are processed entirely by
  [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy); we see subscription status and the
  email you give Stripe, never card numbers.
- **No message content from ordinary channels.** The bots only process
  messages sent *to them* (slash commands, the ask channel, DMs).
- No tracking pixels, no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers.

## How long we keep it

Audit logs (who asked what, when, and moderation records) are kept only as
long as needed for operating and protecting the Service, then compacted or
deleted. Cancelling your subscription removes your subscriber role;
you may additionally request deletion of your stored interaction data
(see below).

## Who we share it with

**Nobody**, except: Discord (the platform the Service runs on), Stripe
(payments), and where required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade any
user data — ever.

## AI processing

Some replies are generated by a **locally hosted** language model on the
operator's own hardware. Your questions to the bots are processed on that
machine; they are **not** sent to third-party AI providers.

## Your choices

- **Access / deletion:** email admin@ariasolutions.org (or ask in
  `#order-here`) to request a copy or deletion of interaction data tied to
  your Discord ID.
- **DMs:** don't want the welcome DM? Disable DMs from server members;
  everything works without it.
- Leaving the server ends all live data collection about you.

## Children

The Service follows Discord's minimum age requirements and is not directed
at children below them.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in `#announcements`
with a new effective date.
